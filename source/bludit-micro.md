*Micro (μ)* is a minimalist, responsive theme for [Bludit](https://www.bludit.com/).

Made for those people that want to cut out the noise and focus on the important part - the content.

- 1 file for Home and Pages.
- Categories are mentioned but are not used.
- Tags are not used.
- 666 bytes of CSS.
- HTML only with `?textonly`.
- Responsive.
- Dark mode.
