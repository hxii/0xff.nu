Another year passed and another year begins. This is exactly the time to summarize experiences and set 'goals' for the upcoming year. This is what you'd call ~~new year resolutions~~.

2019 was an interesting year for me for the most part. I started doing [new things](https://paulglushak.com/yotpo-reviews-for-woocommerce), got promoted (largerly, more of the same) and learned that I'm about to become a father. Quite eventful.
## Goals
I don't like setting solid goals for the new year (we all know that fucking "go to the gym" goal, right?) but I do like deciding on a direction that I'd like to work towards, and so I came up with the following things I'd like to focus on in 2020:
- Learn **how** to be a father. Being responsible for a living thing is fucking scary, especially when that living thing **learns** from everything you do.
- Figure out what the fuck I want my next step in my career to be. I *kinda* know what I want, but I'm still at the point where everything is interesting and I can't seem to choose what to focus on.
- Despite the fact I still believe I'm not much of a developer, I'd like to learn a new language (Ruby? Python? Something else?) and improve my knowledge of PHP. Should be good for both me and my employer.
