As you might've noticed, `0xff.nu` looks... different –– It looks like an OS from the 90-s.

The "fake desktop" concept is one I've been enamored with for quite a while, mostly because it looks cool and because of the nostalgic feeling it gives me personally, and it looks like [I'm definitely not the only one](https://simone.computer/#/webdesktops) that feels that way.

The decision to *actually* pursue and implement this was driven by the fact that I wanted to redesign my space on the internet and make it work for me. That is not to say that the previous design didn't, but I did realize that my brain works in more of a "desktop" mode where a certain note can potentially live in more than one space at the same time, or live in none at all.

A bit of a less bound way to look at things, if you will.

This also comes on the heels of my previous entry "[[corp-burnout|Corporate Burnout]]", where I had the difficult realization that I've had enough of the corporate, lie-ridden circle-jerk of a clique. So the current website design is in a way an even bigger "fuck you" to the expected "norms" of what a website is "supposed" to look like, I guess.

In any case, I hope you enjoy your time browsing it! ♥
If you do come across any issues, please let me know via [BSky](https://bsky.app/profile/0xff.nu).
