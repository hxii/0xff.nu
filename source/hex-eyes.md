I gave [[hex]] a mouth. I also gave it ears and an eye. No head, shoulders, knees and toes yet.
## “Are you creating Skynet?”
No. At least I hope not.

The motivation behind this was to externalize `hex` in order for me to be able to leverage the it's capabilities without being hamstrung to Telegram – which is currently the only way to communicate with it.
Telegram is cool and all, but I sometimes can't be bothered (or just can't) to type a message.
