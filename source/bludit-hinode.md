**日の出** (*Hinode*) is a clean, minimal and bright theme for Bludit.

It started it's life as a commission which fell through, so I've decided to share it with everyone.

**Since this was supposed to be a paid commission, if you like the theme, please consider [donating/tipping](https://ko-fi.com/hxii_).**

### Download
You can download Hinode from [Sourcehut](https://git.sr.ht/~hxii/hinode) or [GitHub](https://github.com/hxii/hinode).
