As some of you might know, I have this tendency of randomly coming up with weird, sometimes interesting ideas. `koi.rest` (then so-imaginatively titled "Zen Pond") was added as a task in the beginning of August, and that was also after it just remained in my head for a few days because, well.... ADHD.
![[Pasted image 20260926110829.png]]
Since August 12th and until September 17th I've done nothing, as even when I added this idea I knew this was low priority –– another one of those ideas it'd be fun to make, if only I knew how or had the time to learn.

The latter as you know, is a big, scary trap with ADHD. Could I have made the time and learned? Yes. I really wanted to. Would it be a good idea to do that? Probably not, at least not at this point.
One of the things I became better at recently (especially comparing to since being diagnosed) is resisting the temptation to try to do *everything*, try to learn *everything* right fucking **now**.
Do I still have more interests than free time? Of-fucking-course I do, but it could be worse.

Then, on September 16th (or maybe 17th) I had a shower thought (as you do) –– I could just let this project live in the project graveyard ~~forever~~ for now and nobody benefits from it. Or I could just, you know, ~~summon the unholy powers~~ leverage AI for the things that I don't know and then everyone benefits from my idea.

I had quite an internal battle to be honest –– I wanted to learn how to make the pond, but I also wanted to exercise my creative vision and planning, and I couldn't do both. In the end, the latter was the stronger want and I started work on the pond.

> Make me a cute, zen koi pond with sound and low-key multiplayer. Make no mistakes

You'd probably think this was the prompt I used, and maybe it would even work.
Instead, I created a project in linear, I wrote the definitions for each of the items I wanted and how I wanted them to work, how I wanted them to look and how I wanted them to feel.

I researched about different methods of faking "water" feel, of fish locomotion, the different koi markings. I tried drawing assets (not knowing how to paint or draw, really) that I want to appear, instead of using SVGs.
![[koi-1790409516284.png]]
I just didn't write the code. That's not to say I don't know what the code does –– I read it and I understand some of it, but not all.





My name is Paul. I’m a developer, an explorer and I’ve got ADHD. As of the beginning of August, I’m also unemployed. This last part, along with everything else this past year, has caused me a great deal of stress.

While our balcony zen garden project is yet to be completed, I had an idea to create a virtual one that everyone can use.  
It's an idea that, unfortunately, I’ve been postponing for a while now, mostly because I have no fucking idea how to do it as I don’t know JavaScript, and I don't have the capacity to learn it right now. So I let perfect be the enemy of good and, well... just kept the idea to myself.

Then I said "fuck it" and used AI to make the thing I really **wanted** to make. I realized I didn't want "perfect". I wanted "good enough". I tweaked, added, removed, drew, researched, questioned, tested... I just wasn't the one coding it.  
So now, instead of occupying my brain, it now lives on the internet for others to enjoy.

Yes, there’s something noble about making something entirely on your own, but what good is an idea that just sits in my head?  
So here I am. I made the thing. The weird, little, quiet koi pond.  
The silly project of passion. The little corner of the internet to let strangers watch fish quietly, together.

I hope this pond helps you as much as it helped me.
