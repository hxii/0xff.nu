This simple, tiny, plugin was born out of necessity and will allow you to embed posts in other posts in WordPress.
**Not** available on wordpress.org, [read why](/overkill-compliance).


Two arguments are available:
- `post_id` - Specifies the ID of the post to embed. Loop at your own risk.
- `class` - Specify a custom CSS class for each embed.
<br>Style as `.embed_post.<yourclassname>` and `.embed_post.<yourclassname> .embed_meta`.<br>Two classes are built-in: `default` and `seamless`.
